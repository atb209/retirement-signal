Behind the page

How this page updates

  1. Every weekday morning an automated job downloads the latest prices and interest rates from FRED, the Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis's free data service, and refreshes the "if the month ended today" reading.
  2. After the last trading day of the month, once FRED posts that month's average interest rates (usually the next business day), the official signal locks in for the month.
  3. If the signal changes, the trade happens on the next trading day. Then nothing changes until the next month-end.

Data: FRED, Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis (S&P 500, Nasdaq-100, 10-year Treasury and 3-month T-bill rates).
